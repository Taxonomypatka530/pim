# 📱 pim - Exchange files between your own devices

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Latest_Release-blue.svg)](https://github.com/Taxonomypatka530/pim/releases)

pim lets you send files, text, notes, and tasks between your devices. It works over your local Wi-Fi network. You do not need the internet to move data. Your files stay on your hardware. 

## 🛠 Features

*   **File Transfer:** Move photos, documents, and videos between your phone and computer.
*   **Text and Notes:** Share clipboard items or quick notes instantly.
*   **Task Management:** Keep your to-do lists in sync across devices.
*   **Offline Operation:** The app works without an internet connection. 
*   **Peer-to-Peer:** Data travels directly between your devices. No server stores your information.

## 💻 System Requirements

You need a Windows computer to use this application. Ensure your computer meets these basic requirements:

*   **Operating System:** Windows 10 or Windows 11.
*   **Network:** Both the computer and the mobile device must connect to the same local Wi-Fi network.
*   **Storage:** At least 200 megabytes of free space on your hard drive.
*   **Memory:** 4 gigabytes of RAM or more.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Once you install the software, connect your devices. 

1.  Open the pim application on your Windows computer.
2.  Ensure your Android phone runs the pim mobile application.
3.  Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi router. 
4.  The application finds other devices on the network automatically. 
5.  Select the device you want to share with from the list.
6.  Choose the file, text, or note you want to send.
7.  Press the send button. 

The data moves directly from one device to the other over the air. The process finishes in seconds depending on the file size.

## 🛡 Network and Security

pim respects your privacy. The app keeps all data within your home or office Wi-Fi network. No third-party servers see your files. Because the app uses peer-to-peer technology, it works as fast as your local network allows. 

If the app fails to find a device, check these items:

*   Does the phone use the same Wi-Fi network as the computer?
*   Do you have a firewall enabled on the computer? You might need to allow pim to communicate through the private network settings.
*   Are both apps open and running in the foreground?

## 💡 Common Questions

**Does this app use the internet?**
No, it only uses your local Wi-Fi signals. Your data never leaves your home network.

**Is my data encrypted?**
Yes, the connection between devices uses encryption. This protects your data during transfer over the air.

**Can I send files between two computers?**
The current version focuses on Windows and Android. You can use it to sync between a Windows computer and an Android device.

**What happens if I turn off the Wi-Fi?**
The devices will lose the connection. You must stay on the same network to exchange items.

**Do I need a user account?**
No, you do not need to create an account. Simply open the app and start sharing.

## 📋 Managing Tasks

You can use the task feature to keep track of work or personal items. When you add a task on your computer, the mobile app shows the update once the devices sync. This provides a way to carry your lists with you when you leave your desk. To add a task, click the plus icon and type your note. Hit save to send it to all paired devices.

## 📁 File Handling

Transfer large files with ease. The app handles photos, PDFs, and documents. When you receive a file, the app saves it in a default folder on your desktop. You can change this path in the settings menu.

## ⚙️ Settings

Click the gear icon to open settings. Here you can change the name of your device. Assigning a clear name helps you identify the sender or receiver easily when multiple devices exist on the network. You can also view the version number of the app in this menu.
